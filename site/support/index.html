How we can help

Voxa is your AI life companion for conversation, reflection, planning, goals, routines, reminders, memories, personalisation, and Journey features. The topics below cover the most common questions for Voxa 1.0.

Contact support

Email: mujimoh2008@gmail.com

When writing in, include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. You do not need email access to delete your account — deletion is available inside the app.

For App Store / EAS release builds, set EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL=mujimoh2008@gmail.com so You → Contact support opens this same inbox.

Getting started

  • Create an account or continue with the onboarding flow in the app.
  • Open Talk to chat with your companion.
  • Use Home and Journey for daily check-ins, goals, reflections, and growth features.
  • Open You for companion settings, privacy, and support.

Account and sign-in

  • Make sure you are connected to the internet when signing in with cloud sync enabled.
  • Check that your email and password are correct. Use the in-app password reset flow if available.
  • If you previously deleted your account, you will need to create a new one.

Talk and AI replies

  • Voxa requires a network connection for AI replies when cloud sync and the AI gateway are enabled.
  • Replies are generated through a server-side AI gateway (not a web chat on this site).
  • AI responses can be inaccurate or incomplete. Verify important information independently.
  • Voxa is an AI companion — not a doctor, therapist, lawyer, financial adviser, or emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Memories

  • Review or delete memories from the Memory area in the app.
  • You can turn memory off from the You tab under Companion settings.
  • Some data may stay on your device only; cloud-synced data is stored with your account when sign-in is enabled.

Goals, routines, and reminders

  • Create and manage goals from Home / Journey flows in the app.
  • Routines and reminders can schedule local notifications on your device when you enable them.
  • If a reminder does not fire, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Voxa, and confirm the feature is enabled in the app.

Notifications

  • Daily check-ins, routines, and goal-linked reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Turn notifications off from the relevant settings in You / companion settings, or from iOS Settings.
  • Voxa 1.0 does not use ads or marketing push networks for these reminders.

Photos, camera, and permissions

  • If photo capture fails, check that Voxa has Camera permission in iOS Settings.
  • Location is optional and used for weather when you enable it. You can choose a city manually instead.
  • Microphone-based features are not required for standard text chat in the current App Store release.

Privacy

  • Read the Privacy Policy for how conversations, memories, attachments, notifications, and cloud sync work.
  • Export available account information from You → Privacy & data → Export data.
  • For privacy requests such as access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact mujimoh2008@gmail.com.

Delete your account

In the Voxa app:

  1. Open the You tab.
  2. Go to Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm the alert.

When cloud sync is enabled and you are signed in, this permanently deletes your authentication account and associated cloud records (including profile, conversations, messages, chat attachments, memories, goals, and reminders), then signs you out and clears local data on your device. If the request fails (for example due to a network error), your account is not deleted — try again when you have a connection. Successful deletion cannot be undone.

Technical issues

  • Force-quit and reopen the app, then confirm you are on a supported iOS version.
  • Check your network connection for Talk / sign-in / sync features.
  • When contacting support, include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
