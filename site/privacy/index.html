Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Who operates Voxa

Voxa is a personal AI companion iOS app for conversation, reflection, planning, goals, routines, reminders, memories, personalisation, and Journey features. This Privacy Policy describes what information is processed when you use Voxa, why it is processed, and the choices available to you.

Voxa is operated by T3Pixel LTD.

Privacy and support contact: mujimoh2008@gmail.com. You can also use You → Contact support in the app.

Information you provide

Depending on how you use Voxa, you may provide:

  • Account credentials and email address (if you create an account)
  • Display name and profile or companion preferences
  • Onboarding answers
  • Chat messages (Talk conversations)
  • Memories you create or ask Voxa to keep
  • Notes, journal, or reflection content
  • Goals and reminders
  • Routine and check-in information
  • Optional faith or values content if you enable that feature
  • Optional nutrition entries if you use calorie tracking
  • Photos or media you attach in chat
  • Support messages you send us

Information collected automatically

When you use Voxa, we may process:

  • Authentication identifiers and session tokens stored on your device
  • App version and basic technical data needed to operate the service
  • Optional device location or a city you choose manually for weather
  • Usage metadata such as AI request counts and token totals when cloud sync and the AI gateway are enabled
  • Locally scheduled notification preferences (for example daily check-ins, routines, or reminders you enable)

Voxa 1.0 does not use third-party advertising networks, does not show ads, and does not sell your conversations.

AI conversation processing

Before Voxa sends personal information to OpenAI, the app asks you to allow or decline AI data sharing. Nothing is preselected. You choose the message you write and any photo you attach. If you allow sharing, Voxa also selects and sends recent conversation history and relevant saved context for that turn: display name, timezone, companion preferences, onboarding answers, memories, goals, reminders, and notes such as mood, check-in, journal, nutrition, or faith entries. Those selected details are not a separate message you typed in that turn. The transfer goes to OpenAI at api.openai.com through Voxa’s Supabase backend so OpenAI can generate a reply.

Ordinary replies use OpenAI’s chat completions endpoint with gpt-4o-mini or gpt-4o. That request sets store to false. OpenAI’s API reference describes that setting as not storing the completion for distillation or evaluation products. It does not stop OpenAI from processing the request, and it is not zero data retention. Some questions use the responses endpoint with a web-search tool. That request can include an approximate country derived from your timezone, and it also sets store to false. OpenAI’s data-controls guide says that avoids keeping the web-search response as application state. It does not skip abuse-monitoring logs. The iOS app does not embed a production OpenAI API key. AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete.

On 27 October 2026, the OpenAI organisation that owns Voxa’s production API key had these controls turned off: model feedback sharing, evaluation and fine-tuning data sharing, and sharing API inputs and outputs to improve models. Turning those controls off means those sharing programmes are not enabled. It does not mean OpenAI skips processing, and it does not mean OpenAI never stores the content it receives. In the same settings, audit logging was not enabled, and API call logging was set to enabled per call. Voxa has not confirmed what that per-call log contains or how long OpenAI keeps it. Voxa has no separate retention agreement with OpenAI, and these settings are not zero data retention.

OpenAI’s platform documentation, reviewed on 27 October 2026, says abuse-monitoring logs for the chat, responses, and speech endpoints may be kept for up to 30 days unless the law requires longer. Image inputs can be kept for review if a safety classifier flags them. OpenAI’s privacy policy is at https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. Its platform data controls are at https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data. Its Services Agreement and Data Processing Addendum are at https://openai.com/policies/services-agreement and https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum. Using the API is how a customer accepts those published terms. Voxa has not recorded a separately signed order form or retention amendment.

Memory functionality

Voxa can store memories you create or ask it to remember. You can review, pin, edit, or delete individual memories in the app. You can turn memory features off in Settings. Memories may be stored on your device and, when cloud sign-in is enabled, synced to your account in cloud storage.

Local vs cloud storage

Some Voxa data stays on your device only (for example notes, mood journal entries, daily reflections, routines, and nutrition logs in the current version).

If you sign in with cloud sync enabled, account-linked data such as profile information, chat messages, memories, goals, and reminders may be stored on secure cloud infrastructure operated by our service providers (including Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage). Data may also be cached on your device for speed and offline use. Settings shows whether your data uses cloud sync or stays on this device.

Photos and attachments

If you capture or attach a photo in Talk, the image may be stored with your account when cloud sync is enabled, and attachment metadata may be stored with the message. If AI data sharing is allowed and you send that photo in the conversation, the image is included in the same request to OpenAI so Voxa can describe it.

Voice and spoken replies

Spoken replies are on unless you turn them off. When AI data sharing is allowed and cloud speech is configured, the text of the reply is sent to OpenAI’s speech endpoint through Voxa’s Supabase backend so OpenAI can create the audio. The published speech reference does not include a store setting, so Voxa does not send one. OpenAI’s data-controls guide lists no application-state retention for that endpoint and lists abuse-monitoring retention of up to 30 days. A voice preview uses a fixed introduction. If you have not allowed AI data sharing, that preview is spoken on the device with Apple’s on-device speech and is not sent to OpenAI. If cloud speech is not configured, Apple’s on-device speech speaks the reply and the text is not sent to OpenAI.

Voxa 1.0 does not record the microphone for chat, voice notes, or live calls. Live voice calls are off in this release. Microphone access is not required for standard text chat or spoken replies.

Location and weather

Location is optional. You may allow device location, choose a city manually, or skip weather features. Location is not required for chat.

If you allow device location, Voxa reads it while you are using the app. Voxa may send the latitude and longitude returned by the device to Open-Meteo at api.open-meteo.com to request a forecast. Those coordinates can be precise. Voxa does not round them to a city or region before sending them. That weather request does not include your Voxa account id. Voxa also stores the coordinates and the resulting place name on your device so it can show and refresh the forecast.

If you type a city instead, Voxa sends that place name to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service, then sends the latitude and longitude returned for that place to the forecast service.

Open-Meteo’s terms, reviewed on 29 October 2026, say that logs for the free API may contain geographical coordinates, are not shared with other parties, and are deleted after 90 days. Those terms also say Open-Meteo does not link IP addresses from the free API to user identities. Voxa has not inspected those logs and has no separate agreement with Open-Meteo. This policy does not say that coordinates are deleted as soon as the forecast is returned.

If you have allowed AI data sharing and you ask about weather, a city name, or a country derived from your timezone, may be included in the AI request described above. That request does not include the raw device coordinates.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Voxa schedules local reminders and check-ins on your device (for example daily check-ins, routines, or goal-linked reminders). Notification content is generated and delivered locally. You can change preferences in the app and in iOS Settings.

Third-party service providers

We use service providers to operate Voxa, including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and file storage when cloud sync is enabled; server-side functions including the AI gateway, speech gateway, and account deletion
  • OpenAI — reply generation, photo description, and speech audio when you allow AI data sharing
  • Open-Meteo (or a configured weather proxy) — forecasts when weather is enabled
  • Apple — App Store distribution, on-device speech when cloud speech is unavailable or a voice preview is played without AI data sharing, and local notifications
  • Expo / EAS — app build and updates infrastructure

Voxa 1.0 is completely free — no subscriptions, ads, paid upgrades, or in-app purchases are offered in this release. Voxa does not send conversations to an advertising network. Supabase publishes a Data Processing Addendum at https://supabase.com/legal/customer-resources/data-processing-addendum. Voxa has not recorded a separately signed Supabase order in this policy. Voxa does not control OpenAI’s later handling of content it receives. The organisation settings described above are the controls checked on 27 October 2026. They are not zero data retention and they are not a promise that OpenAI never stores user content.

Why we process information

We process information to: provide and personalise the companion experience; remember what you ask us to remember; sync your account when cloud sign-in is enabled; deliver optional features you turn on; operate notifications you request; protect against abuse; and keep the service working reliably.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal conversations. If you allow AI data sharing, we send the categories described above to OpenAI through our backend so OpenAI can generate a reply or speech audio. We also share information with the other service providers listed above so they can perform the service you asked for, when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger or acquisition.

You can decline AI data sharing or withdraw it later in You → Privacy & data → AI data sharing. On the device where you do that, later transfers stop immediately. Other devices are blocked after the account update is saved, the next time they reach Voxa. Withdrawal does not recall a request that has already been sent, and it does not reach other devices while this device is offline.

Retention

Voxa keeps account information while the account is active, including the AI data-sharing choice stored for that account. Voxa’s own usage record for an AI request stores counts, such as token totals and character counts, and does not store the message text. Cached data on your device remains until you delete it, sign out with local cleanup, or delete your account. Deleting the account removes the account’s cloud records that the deletion service is built to remove, including the consent record, once that record exists. This policy does not promise an OpenAI retention period. The organisation controls checked on 27 October 2026 turn off the listed sharing programmes and do not enable audit logging. API call logging was enabled per call. Voxa has no zero-data-retention approval and no separate retention agreement. OpenAI’s platform documentation reviewed on 27 October 2026 describes abuse-monitoring logs of up to 30 days for the chat, responses, and speech endpoints Voxa uses, unless the law requires longer, and says image inputs can be kept if a safety classifier flags them. Those documents also say chat completions are not kept as application state except for the documented image, audio-output, and prompt-cache exceptions, and that a responses request with store set to false is not kept as application state. None of this means OpenAI never stores the content it receives.

Your choices and rights

You can review memories, export some of your data, delete individual memories, turn spoken replies off, control optional permissions in iOS Settings, and request account deletion from the app (You → Privacy & data). AI data sharing is off until you choose Allow AI data sharing. You can withdraw it from You → Privacy & data → AI data sharing. Withdrawal blocks later Talk and speech transfers from that device immediately, and blocks other devices after the account update is saved. Where privacy law gives you a right of access, correction, or deletion, you may request it by contacting mujimoh2008@gmail.com. You may complain to a supervisory authority where the law gives you that right.

Account deletion

You may delete your account from You → Privacy & data → Delete account. When cloud sync is enabled and you are signed in, this requests deletion of your authentication account and associated cloud records (including profile, conversations, messages, chat attachments, memories, goals, and reminders) through our deletion service, then clears local app data on your device. If the deletion request fails (for example due to a network error), your account is not deleted and you can try again. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone once it succeeds. Some provider-side processing logs may persist for a limited period as described by those providers.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including secure token storage on supported devices and access controls on cloud infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International processing

Your information may be processed in countries where we or our service providers operate, including the United States and the European Economic Area depending on provider configuration. Those countries may have different data protection laws than your own.

Children's privacy

Voxa is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). Do not create an account for a child below that age.
